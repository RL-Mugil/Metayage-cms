How it works

From first idea to
granted patent.

A clear path with a decision at every step, so you always know what is being protected, why, and what comes next.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about the technology

    A short conversation about how it works and what makes it different. Keep confidential details high-level at first.

  2. Step 2

    Search and assess

    We review the prior art and consider where protection is realistic, and what to file first.

  3. Step 3

    File with intent

    A provisional application to secure an early date, or a full application, written around your real invention.

  4. Step 4

    Prosecute and build

    Respond to the examiner, shape claims around the business, and keep your IP ready for diligence.
