  4. AUTOMATION BLUEPRINT

  High-Impact Automations (Tier 1: 80% of operational friction)

  A1: Client Case Auto-Population on Project Creation

  - Name: "Case → Project Sync"
  - Trigger: Project created with client_id + case_reference
  - Action:
    - Fetch client's active cases from DB
    - Auto-populate project fields: client_code, contact_email, invoice_address, gst_rate
    - Pre-assign project PM based on client's historical PM (if exists)
    - Generate default folder structure in Documents tab
  - Business Impact:
    - Time Saved: 5-10 min per project (data re-entry eliminated)
    - Error Reduction: 90% fewer GST, contact mismatches
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 50 projects/year × 7 min = 5.8 hours + error cost savings

  ---
  A2: Task → Time Entry → Invoice Auto-Generation

  - Name: "Billable Task Tracking to Invoice"
  - Trigger: Task marked [COMPLETED] OR time entry submitted for billable task
  - Action:
    - Fetch time entries for task (sum hours)
    - Multiply by task's billing_rate → line item amount
    - Group by project → auto-generate invoice draft
    - Add to Financial > Pending Invoices (awaiting review)
    - Email PM: "Invoice ready for review: Acme Corp $4,500"
  - Business Impact:
    - Time Saved: 15-20 min per invoice (no manual line-item entry)
    - Error Reduction: 100% accuracy (no manual math)
    - Revenue Impact: 2-3 day faster invoicing cycle = 5% faster cash collection
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 200 invoices/year × 15 min = 50 hours; 5% faster collection = $20K+ on 1M/year revenue

  ---
  A3: Leave Request → Attendance Auto-Adjustment

  - Name: "Approved Leave to Attendance"
  - Trigger: Leave request approved by manager
  - Action:
    - Mark employee's attendance as [ON_LEAVE] for date range
    - Automatically deduct from leave balance
    - Email employee: "Your leave for Jun 10–12 is approved; attendance updated"
    - Exclude from workload calculations (Team tab)
  - Business Impact:
    - Time Saved: 3 min per approval (no manual attendance entry)
    - Compliance: 100% audit trail (no lost records)
    - HR Efficiency: No manager follow-up needed
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 60 approvals/year × 3 min = 3 hours

  ---
  A4: Overdue Task → Escalation Workflow

  - Name: "Deadline Alert & Escalation"
  - Trigger: Task due date passed + status ≠ [COMPLETED]
  - Action (Escalation Ladder):
    a. T+0 (Due date): Assignee gets Slack/email reminder
    b. T+1 day: Task PM notified; task flagged red in dashboard
    c. T+2 days: Project PM notified; discussion thread auto-created ("Task overdue: [task_name]")
    d. T+3 days: Partner/Manager dashboard alert; auto-escalate in Project status
    e. T+5 days: Auto-create approval request for scope/timeline change
  - Business Impact:
    - Time Saved: 10 min per overdue task (no manual escalation calls)
    - Error Reduction: 0% missed escalations
    - Delivery: Catch delays 3 days earlier → 90% on-time delivery
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 5-10 overdue tasks/year × 10 min = 1 hour; 90% on-time vs 70% = 20% fewer client complaints

  ---
  A5: Client Portal: Invoice → Payment Status Auto-Sync

  - Name: "Payment Gateway to Portal Visibility"
  - Trigger: Payment received via gateway (Stripe, bank API) → marked in Financial module
  - Action:
    - Update invoice status: [PENDING] → [PAID]
    - Add payment timestamp, method, receipt URL
    - Auto-send client portal notification: "Invoice #INV-001 marked paid on Jun 5"
    - Update Financial dashboard: cash collected
  - Business Impact:
    - Transparency: Clients see real-time payment status (reduces support calls)
    - Time Saved: 2 min per payment (no manual email to client)
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 200 payments/year × 2 min = 6.7 hours; 30% fewer "Where's my receipt?" emails

  ---
  A6: Discussion → Task Auto-Creation

  - Name: "Decision to Action Item"
  - Trigger: Team member replies to discussion with tag [TODO: OR discussion marked "requires action"
  - Action:
    - Parse reply text for task description
    - Create task automatically in parent project
    - Assign to mentioned team member (if @name tagged)
    - Add task link back to discussion (two-way reference)
    - Email: "New task created from discussion: [task_name]"
  - Business Impact:
    - Friction Reduction: No context switching (discussion → new tab → task creation)
    - Time Saved: 3 min per task (avoid manual creation)
    - Audit: All decisions traceable to action items
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 20 discussions/month × 3 min = 12 hours

  ---
  A7: Team Workload Auto-Balancing Alerts

  - Name: "Workload Rebalance Recommendation"
  - Trigger: Team member workload > 90% (from Project Tracker analytics) for 3+ consecutive days
  - Action:
    - Flag employee in Team tab (red workload bar)
    - Auto-generate "Rebalancing Recommendation" report:
        - Top 3 tasks could be reassigned
      - Suggested assignee (least busy team member in same role)
    - Email PM: "Alice is overloaded; suggest reassigning [Task X] to Bob?"
    - One-click reassign button (updates Tracker + Team + Discussions)
  - Business Impact:
    - Burnout Prevention: Early detection of overwork
    - Efficiency: Optimal task distribution
    - Time Saved: 5 min per rebalancing (vs manual analysis)
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 10 rebalancing events/year × 5 min = 50 min; reduced turnover cost

  ---
  A8: Report Auto-Generation & Email Scheduling

  - Name: "Scheduled Reports to Executive Inbox"
  - Trigger: User schedules report (e.g., "Weekly Client Status every Monday 6 AM")
  - Action:
    - Vite build generates HTML report file from template
    - Email to recipients (PDF attached or link)
    - Store in Reports > History (read-only)
    - Add to client portal if client-specific
  - Business Impact:
    - Time Saved: 10 min per report (no manual generation)
    - Reliability: Never miss a report deadline
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 4 reports/week × 10 min × 52 weeks = 34.7 hours

  ---
  A9: Client Document Upload → Auto-OCR & Tagging

  - Name: "Smart Document Indexing"
  - Trigger: Client uploads document via portal (or employee uploads to Documents)
  - Action:
    - Run OCR (AWS Textract or similar) to extract text
    - Auto-tag document by content (Invoice, Contract, Notice, etc.)
    - Extract metadata (date, parties, amounts, deadlines)
    - Add to searchable index
    - Link to related projects/tasks (if keywords match)
    - Email: "Document uploaded: [filename] — Found reference to Project X"
  - Business Impact:
    - Knowledge Recovery: Documents searchable by content, not just filename
    - Time Saved: 2 min per document (no manual tagging)
    - Compliance: All documents indexed for audits
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 100 documents/year × 2 min = 3.3 hours; 50% faster document retrieval

  ---
  A10: Approval Routing by Role & SLA

  - Name: "Smart Approval Workflow"
  - Trigger: Approval request created (e.g., Leave, Scope Change, Invoice approval)
  - Action (SLA-Based):
    a. Determine approver: Route to manager if leave; PM if scope; finance if invoice
    b. Set deadline: 1 day for leave; 2 days for scope; 3 days for invoice
    c. Auto-escalate: If not approved by deadline, escalate to next level
    d. Notify chain: Email approver + backup approver
    e. Track: Dashboard shows pending approvals by SLA age
  - Business Impact:
    - Speed: 80% faster approvals (automated routing)
    - Compliance: SLA-driven; audit trail automatic
    - Transparency: Approvers see what's pending
    - Estimated Annual Benefit: 50 approvals/year × 1 day reduction = 50 days faster decisions
