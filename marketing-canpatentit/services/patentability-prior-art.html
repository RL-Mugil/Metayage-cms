Patentability & Prior Art Search

Before you spend on a filing, find out where your idea sits among what has already been published and patented.

Book a consultation Try the self-check

What this is

A patentability review compares your invention with published patents and technical literature. The goal is a clear picture of what looks new, what looks crowded, and which features are worth building a filing around.

No search is exhaustive and no review can promise an outcome at the patent office. What it can do is replace guesswork with context before you commit budget.

How it works

  1. Technical conversation. We start with how the system actually works and what you believe is different.
  2. Search. We look across patent databases and technical literature using the concepts, not only the keywords, of your approach.
  3. Findings. You receive a plain summary of the closest references and where your approach appears to differ.
  4. Next step. We discuss whether to file, what to emphasise, or what to build or disclose differently.

Common questions

Does a search guarantee a patent will be granted?

No. A search informs your decision, but examination by the patent office is a separate process and the outcome cannot be guaranteed.

Should I search before I publish or demo?

Ideally yes. Public disclosure can affect your rights, so speak with us before you publish, present or launch.
