IP Strategy & Diligence

IP should support the business: what you own, what is protectable, where the risks are, and what to do next.

Book a consultation Try the self-check

Strategy that follows the roadmap

We help you decide what to protect, what to keep as a trade secret, what to publish, and in what order, with the product, the market and the runway in view.

The aim is a portfolio that tells a coherent story rather than a pile of unrelated filings.

Diligence readiness

  • A clear inventory of filings, inventions, ownership and assignments
  • Gaps and risks identified before an investor finds them
  • Plain explanations for the questions diligence teams usually ask about IP
  • Freedom-to-operate questions framed so you know what needs deeper analysis

A full freedom-to-operate opinion is a distinct, scoped piece of work; we will tell you when one is warranted.

Common questions

Do you assess whether we infringe someone else's patent?

We can flag areas of concern and recommend when a formal freedom-to-operate analysis makes sense. That is a separate, scoped engagement.

We have no filings yet. Is strategy still useful?

Yes. Early decisions about disclosure, ownership and filing order are often the cheapest to get right.
